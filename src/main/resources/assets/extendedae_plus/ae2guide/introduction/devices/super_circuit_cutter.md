# Super Circuit Cutter

<BlockImage id="extendedae_plus:circuit_cutter_plus" scale="5" />

The **Super Circuit Cutter** processes up to **8 copies** of the same circuit-cutter recipe in a single cycle.

## Operation

- Insert the required item and fluid into the machine.
- The machine automatically processes between 1 and 8 recipes according to available input, fluid, and output space.
- Item inputs, fluid inputs, and AE power are consumed for every parallel operation.
- Outputs are combined in the output slot. When it cannot fit a full batch, the parallel count is reduced automatically.

## Power and Upgrades

The machine stores **64,000 AE** and supports up to 4 AE2 Speed Cards. Speed Cards reduce processing time but do not change the 8-recipe parallel limit.

Enable **Auto-Export** in the machine interface to send finished products to adjacent inventories.

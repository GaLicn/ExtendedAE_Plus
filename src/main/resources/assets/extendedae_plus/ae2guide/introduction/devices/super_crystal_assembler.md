# Super Crystal Assembler

<BlockImage id="extendedae_plus:crystal_assembler_plus" scale="5" />

The **Super Crystal Assembler** processes crystal-assembler recipes at up to **8 parallel operations** per cycle.

## Operation

- Insert recipe items into its 9 input slots and the required fluid into the internal tank.
- A cycle automatically runs between 1 and 8 copies of the same recipe, based on available inputs, fluid, and output space.
- Materials, fluid, and AE power are consumed in proportion to the completed parallel operations.
- The single output slot combines all products. If it cannot hold a full batch, the machine reduces the parallel count automatically.

## Power and Upgrades

The machine has an internal buffer of **64,000 AE** and accepts up to 4 AE2 Speed Cards. Speed Cards shorten processing time; the parallel limit remains 8 recipes per cycle.

Enable **Auto-Export** in the machine interface to send finished products to adjacent inventories.
